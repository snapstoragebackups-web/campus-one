# 🎓 Smart Campus – One Platform for Smarter Campus Life

> **A Modern, Unified, AI-Powered Campus Operating System for Students and Administrators**  
> *Designed for Hackathons & Next-Generation Smart University Campuses.*

---

## 🌟 Executive Overview & Problem Statement

Modern college campuses are notoriously fragmented. Students and staff have to navigate across:
- **Paper registers & physical logbooks** for hostel entry, gate-passes, and leaves.
- **Scattered WhatsApp groups & unofficial chat threads** for timetable changes, announcements, and mess updates.
- **Physical bulletin boards** where critical fee deadlines and exam schedules get buried or missed.
- **Disconnected administrative silos** requiring physical visits to the registrar, accounts department, and hostel wardens for signatures and stamps.
- **Unclear grievance resolution**, where maintenance complaints (electricity, Wi-Fi, plumbing) remain unassigned for weeks without status visibility.

**Smart Campus (Campus One)** unites these fragmented touchpoints into **one connected ecosystem** bridging students, faculty, hostel wardens, and university leadership.

---

## ⚡ The Connected Workflow Engine

Every interaction flows across a closed-loop automated pipeline:

```
[ Student Submits Request ]
            │
            ▼
[ System / AI Categorizes & Assigns Priority ]
            │
            ▼
[ Relevant Department Receives Ticket in Admin Hub ]
            │
            ▼
[ Admin / Warden Takes Action (Approve / Reject / Assign / Relocate) ]
            │
            ▼
[ Student Receives Instant Toast, Ding & Notification Badge ]
            │
            ▼
[ Status Progresses & Updates Central Analytics in Real Time ]
```

## 🚀 Key Modules & Feature Highlights

### 1. 🎓 Student Dashboard
- **Personalized Header**: Roll Number, Program, Year, CGPA, Hostel & Room Number.
- **Critical Risk Alert**: Highlights any subject under the 75% statutory threshold with exact classes required to attend.
- **Next Lecture Card**: Live room number, instructor, and relocation alerts.
- **Active Gate-Pass**: Quick-access QR badge with countdown to curfew (8:30 PM).
- **Active Hostel Maintenance Tracker**: Live progress from *Submitted* to *Resolved*.
- **Today's Dining Snapshot**: Lunch and dinner menu with hygiene rating.
- **Instant Student Services**: Fast-action buttons for one-tap applications.

### 2. 🤖 24/7 AI Campus Assistant
- **Natural Language Inquiry Engine**: Handles questions about attendance risk, timetable, fees, mess food, rules, and circulars.
- **Interactive Process Guides**:
  1. *How to apply for a Gate Pass*
  2. *How to request a Bonafide Certificate*
  3. *Medical Attendance Condonation Procedure*
- **Auto-Department Escalation**: When automated guidance is insufficient, students can trigger an official escalation ticket directly to the Dean or Warden with a 2-hour SLA.

### 3. 🛠️ Smart Hostel Complaint System
- **Automated Categorization**: Wi-Fi & Internet, Plumbing & Water, Electricity & Power, Cleanliness, Furniture, AC & Fans.
- **AI Priority Detection**: Automatically assigns *Urgent*, *High*, *Medium*, or *Low* priority based on critical keywords (e.g., "sparking", "leakage", "flood", "exam").
- **Photo Evidence Attachment**: Includes preset test evidence and custom upload support.
- **4-Step Visual Progression Stepper**: `Submitted` ➔ `Assigned` ➔ `In Progress` ➔ `Resolved`.
- **Technician Profile & Dispatch Tracking**: Shows technician name and real-time updates.
- **Post-Resolution Rating**: Students rate technician performance (1–5 stars) upon resolution.
- **Recurring Issue Cluster Detection**: Flags recurring floor-wide issues (e.g. Wi-Fi drops on Block B 3rd Floor).

### 4. 📊 Smart Attendance Alerts & "What-If" Simulator
- **Real-Time Subject Attendance**: Attended vs. total delivered lectures.
- **Safe-to-Miss Calculator**: Calculates how many upcoming lectures a student can safely skip without dropping below 75%.
- **Debarment Warning Calculator**: Calculates how many consecutive lectures a student must attend to cross the safe 75% boundary.
- **Interactive "What-If" Predictive Simulator**: Sliders allowing students to test future attendance scenarios and project their end-semester percentage.

### 5. 📅 Smart Timetable & Live Broadcasts
- **Personalized Day-Wise Schedule**: Complete weekly timetable with instructor and room location.
- **Live Status Badges**: *Completed*, *Live Now* (with pulsing green badge), and *Upcoming*.
- **Room Relocation Alerts**: Highlights when lectures shift halls (e.g. OS relocated to CS Lab 4).
- **Class Reminders Toggle**: Audio and push notification toggles.
- **Admin Class Relocation Tool**: Enables administrators to broadcast sudden hall changes campus-wide.

### 6. 🚪 Digital Gate-Pass System
- **Digital Leave Application**: Weekend visits, medical trips, and hackathon participation.
- **Dynamic QR Code**: Cryptographically encoded gate pass with out/in time windows.
- **Campus Security Guard Terminal Simulator**: Simulates the guard checkpoint at Main Gate 1 scanning the QR pass to grant or deny access.
- **Official Printable Out-Pass**: Full printable credentials with student details and warden authorization.

### 7. 📜 Digital Certificate Request Portal
- **Supported Certificates**: Bonafide Certificate, Tuition Fee Paid Receipt, Character & Conduct Certificate, Course Completion, and Internship NOC.
- **Official Certificate Generator**: Generates authentic university letterheads featuring:
  - Official university seal & watermarks.
  - Verifiable registration serial numbers (e.g. `CAMPUS/ACAD/2026/BF-8910`).
  - Cryptographic QR code verification.
  - Digital signature of Registrar & Controller of Examinations.
  - One-tap browser print / PDF export.

### 8. 🍲 Mess Management & Dining
- **Weekly Multi-Cuisine Menu**: Detailed schedules for Monday through Sunday covering Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner.
- **Dietary & Nutrition Highlights**: Special counters, veg/non-veg indicators, salad bars.
- **Daily Student Ratings & Reviews Feed**: 5-star rating system with student commentary.
- **Mess Hygiene & Compliance Index**: 96% FSSAI Grade A compliance tracking.
- **One-Tap Mess Issue Reporting**: Direct ticket creation for food quality or dining hall cleanliness.

### 9. 📢 Smart Notice & Reminder System
- **Categorized Circulars**: Academic, Exams, Fees, Hostel, Events.
- **Targeted Audiences**: Broadcasts specifically to *All Students*, *Hostel Residents*, or *Department-specific* cohorts.
- **Automated Deadline Countdowns**: Highlights urgent deadlines (e.g. Term fee Oct 15, Mid-Terms Oct 18).
- **Read / Unread Receipt Management**: Lets students mark notices as acknowledged.

### 10. 💳 Fee Accounts & FastPay Simulator
- **Account Balances**: Total fees, amount paid, and outstanding balances.
- **Itemized Ledger**: Tuition, Hostel, Mess, Library, and Exam Fees.
- **Instant FastPay Gateway Simulator**: UPI, NetBanking, and Card simulation with immediate receipt reference generation (`TXN-xxxxxx`).
- **Official Fee Receipts**: Downloadable tax-deductible payment receipts (Sec 80E eligible).

### 11. 🚨 Emergency Assistance & Satellite SOS
- **One-Tap Panic SOS**: Broadcasts GPS coordinates (Lat 18.5204° N, Long 73.8567° E - Hostel Block B) to Campus Security Control and dispatches the on-campus ICU ambulance.

### 12. 🌐 Multi-Language & Accessibility (Low-Bandwidth Mode)
- **Regional Languages**: English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்), and Marathi (मराठी).

### 13. 🛡️ Administrator Command Hub & Analytics

  - Academic certificate e-signing and serial generation.
- **Visual Analytics**: Complaint category distribution and department workload queues.
- **Emergency Schedule Broadcaster**: Instant broadcast of lecture relocation to all enrolled students.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend Core**: React 18, Vite 6
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 3.4 (custom campus color palette, glassmorphism, responsive breakpoints)
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **QR Codes**: `qrcode.react` (cryptographic payload encoding)
- **Interactive FX**: `canvas-confetti` (for approval celebrations)
- **Persistence**: Browser `localStorage` state sync for zero-friction hackathon demos
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts)
